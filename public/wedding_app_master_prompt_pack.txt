   - Program Schedule (ProgramSchedule.jsx) featuring Groom events with mobile aspect ratio "w-full h-auto object-contain".
   - Kids' Special Invitation Card (BalSandesh.jsx) ("[KIDS_NAMES] invitation").
   - Venue Location & Directions (LocationsSection.jsx) with embedded Google Maps iframe and "Get Directions" link.
   - Travel & Accommodation Guide (TravelGuide.jsx) with railway, road, and airport instructions.
   - AI Doodle Gallery (GallerySection.jsx).
   - Attendance Confirmation (RSVPSection.jsx) with inline green success card.
   - Guestbook Blessings Wall (WishesWall.jsx).
   - Footer (Footer.jsx) with WhatsApp share invitation trigger.

3. Bride Page (BridePage.jsx):
   - Mirror structure of Groom Page tuned for [BRIDE_HOSTS] and [BRIDE_VENUE].
